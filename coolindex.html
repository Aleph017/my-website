How this website works

It's hosted on my old Asus K52F laptop. It runs Arch Linux, nginx to serve HTML, CSS and JS. I use myaddr.io and duckdns.org for dynamic DNS. The traffic passes through whole 2 routers at my home; I have to keep this unholy setup because of the legacy TV hardware my family uses.

My laptop's uptime is scheduled, so this website is accessible only 4AM-11PM UTC, because of my bedtime. If you're on time but the site is still inaccessible, then my ISP has put me behind their CGNAT.
